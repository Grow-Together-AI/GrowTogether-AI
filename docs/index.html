          About Grow Together AI

          🎯

          Mission

          To give parents clear, trustworthy, evidence-based guidance that helps children ages 6–12 grow into confident, capable people — one small step at a time.

          🔭

          Vision

          A world where every family has access to warm, personalized developmental support — not just families who can afford private specialists.

          🤖

          How AI Works

          Grow Together AI reviews the information a parent shares, matches it against trusted developmental guidance, and turns that into a personalized weekly plan and report — always educational, never diagnostic.

          📚

          Evidence-Based Methodology

          Recommendations are grounded in guidance from the World Health Organization, CDC, American Academy of Pediatrics, UNICEF, and the Center on the Developing Child at Harvard University.

          🧒

          Supported Age Group

          Ages 6–12 — from early school age through the transition toward adolescence.

          🔒

          Privacy Principles

          Family information is used only to personalize guidance, is never sold, and families can review or remove it at any time. This prototype does not store any real data.
